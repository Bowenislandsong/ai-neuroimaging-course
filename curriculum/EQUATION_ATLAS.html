R00 · Read for the question before the equations — The Evidence-to-Claim Decomposition

Breaks any published neuroimaging conclusion into three multiplicative logical gates: if any single stage fails, the downstream claim collapses.

\[\text{Claim Validity} = \underbrace{\text{Measurement Fidelity}}_{\text{Physics \& Metadata}} \times \underbrace{\text{Transformation Contract}}_{\text{Invariants Preserved}} \times \underbrace{\text{Out-of-Sample Generalization}}_{\text{Independent Evaluation}}\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (R00):
Notice how empirical measurement sits between a theoretical question and a falsifiable claim. In Pass 1–2 of reading a neuroimaging paper, map the main figure's axes, physical units, and transformation chain before reading the authors' narrative interpretation.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(\text{Measurement Fidelity}\)Biophysical Input GateCaptures what the scanner physically recorded (TR, TE, voxel size, B0 field) versus what was lost before software ever touched the file.Confusing BOLD T2* hemodynamic contrast with direct millisecond neuronal spiking.
\(\text{Transformation Contract}\)Mathematical Operator GateSpecifies the exact domain, codomain, fitted parameters, preserved invariants, and destroyed degrees of freedom of the processing/modeling pipeline.Applying a black-box script that silently alters coordinate frames or smooths across tissue boundaries.
\(\text{Out-of-Sample Generalization}\)Inferential Boundary GateTests whether the estimated effect or prediction survives on unseen participants and independent scanners without data leakage.Reporting in-sample or circularly selected statistics as generalizable population truth.

R01 · Why does a scan need a processing workflow? — Composite Spatial Preprocessing Operator

Chains every spatial transformation into a single composite pullback mapping so voxel intensities are interpolated only once rather than blurred repeatedly.

\[I_{\text{analysis}}(\mathbf{x}) = \left(\mathcal{R}_{\text{interp}} \circ T_{\text{MNI}\leftarrow\text{T1w}} \circ T_{\text{T1w}\leftarrow\text{EPI}} \circ T_{\text{SDC}} \circ T_{\text{motion}, t}\right)\!\big[I_{\text{raw}}(\mathbf{i}, t)\big]\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (R01):
Raw functional and structural scans contain head motion, susceptibility warping, intensity bias, and arbitrary scanner coordinates. A transparent processing pipeline aligns anatomy and function while exposing visual quality-control checkpoints at every stage.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(I_{\text{raw}}(\mathbf{i}, t)\)Raw Scanner TensorThe discrete 4D integer/float array on the scanner's native voxel lattice i=(i,j,k) at frame t.Treating raw voxel indices as matched anatomical locations across time or participants.
\(T_{\text{SDC}} \circ T_{\text{motion}, t}\)Within-Run Rigid + Distortion Transforms6-DOF head motion realignment composed with phase-encoding B0 susceptibility unwarping.Ignoring residual spin-history and B0-by-motion interactions after rigid realignment.
\(T_{\text{MNI}\leftarrow\text{T1w}} \circ T_{\text{T1w}\leftarrow\text{EPI}}\)Cross-Modal & Template TransformsBoundary-based EPI-to-T1w registration composed with nonlinear diffeomorphic warp to standard template space.Resampling after each individual step, which compounds interpolation smoothing.
\(\mathcal{R}_{\text{interp}}\)Single-Shot Resampling KernelEvaluates the continuous interpolation kernel (Lanczos/B-spline for images, nearest-neighbor for integer labels) once on the target grid.Using linear interpolation on discrete parcellation labels, inventing blended ROI integers.

R02 · When will a brain result work for a new person? — Attenuated Out-of-Sample Brain-Wide Association Correlation

Shows why observed brain-behavior correlations are bounded by measurement reliability and why small-N studies suffer massive sampling dispersion.

\[r_{\text{obs}} = r_{\text{true}} \sqrt{\text{ICC}_X \cdot \text{ICC}_Y} + \varepsilon_{\text{sample}}(N)\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (R02):
In K-fold and multi-site cross-validation, every preprocessing parameter, feature screen, and model weight must be estimated strictly inside the training folds (blue) and evaluated once on held-out participants/sites (orange).
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(r_{\text{true}}\)Latent Biological AssociationThe true population correlation between the noiseless neural trait and the noiseless behavioral phenotype.Assuming a small latent effect (e.g., r=0.10) will look large in a small sample without winner's curse inflation.
\(\sqrt{\text{ICC}_X \cdot \text{ICC}_Y}\)Reliability Attenuation FactorGeometric mean of brain-measure test-retest reliability (ICC_X) and behavioral reliability (ICC_Y), shrinking the observable correlation.Using noisy short-scan resting-state edges (low ICC_X) that cap observable correlation near zero.
\(\varepsilon_{\text{sample}}(N)\)Finite-Sample Estimation NoiseSampling error of order O(1/sqrt(N)) that causes small samples (N=25) to produce wildly inflated or sign-reversed significant hits.Thresholding small-N correlations at p<0.05, which guarantees high Type M (magnitude) and Type S (sign) errors.

R03 · Which choices stand between a result and a claim? — Total Variance Across Analytical Pipelines (Multiverse Decomposition)

Decomposes total uncertainty into standard within-pipeline sampling error plus the hidden variance across defensible analysis pipelines.

\[\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\theta}) = \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{\pi}\!\left[\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\theta} \mid \pi)\right]}_{\text{Sampling Variance Within Pipeline}} + \underbrace{\operatorname{Var}_{\pi}\!\left(\mathbb{E}[\hat{\theta} \mid \pi]\right)}_{\text{Pipeline / Vibration Variance Across Choices}}\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (R03):
When 70 independent research teams analyze the exact same neuroimaging dataset (NARPS), legitimate variations in smoothing, motion modeling, and thresholding create a forest of divergent effect estimates. Auditing evidence requires separating data signal from researcher degrees of freedom.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\theta})\)Total Claim UncertaintyTrue total variance of the reported scientific estimate across both participant sampling and analytical branches.Reporting only the first term and pretending the pipeline choice had zero impact.
\(\mathbb{E}_{\pi}[\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\theta} \mid \pi)]\)Conventional Standard Error TermAverage sampling variance printed by software assuming a single fixed pipeline pi.Treating a narrow single-pipeline p-value as proof that the scientific conclusion is robust.
\(\operatorname{Var}_{\pi}(\mathbb{E}[\hat{\theta} \mid \pi])\)Analytical Vibration VarianceDispersion of the expected estimate across valid preprocessing, design, and inference specifications (NARPS).Cherry-picking the single pipeline branch pi* that maximizes statistical significance.

F01 · What the AI does and what you must understand — Explicit Transformation Contract Specification

Defines every neuroimaging code step as a typed mathematical mapping with explicit array dimensions, physical units, coordinate frame, and training-only fit scope.

\[\mathcal{T}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}: \mathcal{X}_{(d_1 \times \dots \times d_k)}^{[\text{units}, \text{space}]} \longrightarrow \mathcal{Y}_{(m_1 \times \dots \times m_r)}^{[\text{units}', \text{space}']}, \quad \boldsymbol{\theta} = \operatorname{fit}\!\left(\mathcal{X}_{\text{train}}\right)\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (F01):
In agentic neuroimaging workflows, the AI coding assistant (Goose + Qwen) translates your explicit mathematical contract into executable code, while you (backed by Gemma 4 auditing and numerical assertions) verify invariants, axes, and physical units.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(\mathcal{X}_{(d_1 \times \dots \times d_k)}^{[\text{units}, \text{space}]}\)Domain & Input MetadataInput array with named axis dimensions (e.g., X,Y,Z,T), physical units (e.g., mm, s, arb. intensity), and coordinate space (e.g., native RAS+).Passing unnamed NumPy arrays where voxel and time axes are silently transposed.

F02 · From tissue to MRI contrast to BOLD — Spoiled Gradient-Echo (GRE / EPI) Steady-State Signal Equation

Governs how sequence timing (TR, TE, flip angle theta) interacts with tissue relaxation (T1, T2*) to produce anatomical contrast and functional BOLD sensitivity.

\[S_{\text{GRE}}(\text{TR}, \text{TE}, \theta) \propto \underbrace{\rho}_{\text{Spin Density}} \cdot \underbrace{\frac{\sin\theta\left(1 - e^{-\text{TR}/T_1}\right)}{1 - \cos\theta\, e^{-\text{TR}/T_1}}}_{\text{Steady-State } T_1 \text{ \& Flip-Angle Gain}} \cdot \underbrace{e^{-\text{TE}/T_2^*}}_{\text{BOLD } T_2^* \text{ Dephasing Decay}}\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (F02):
Compare the rising longitudinal recovery curve M_z(t) = M_0(1 - e^{-t/T1}) against the decaying transverse coherence curve M_xy(t) = M_0 e^{-t/T2}. Repetition Time (TR) samples the T1 recovery curve; Echo Time (TE) samples the T2/T2* decay curve.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(\rho\)Equilibrium Proton DensityBaseline concentration of mobile 1H water/lipid spins setting the maximum available magnetization M0.Mistaking raw voxel intensity for absolute proton density without accounting for T1, T2*, and coil bias.
\(\frac{\sin\theta(1 - e^{-\text{TR}/T_1})}{1 - \cos\theta\,e^{-\text{TR}/T_1}}\)Longitudinal T1 Recovery & Ernst GainDetermines how much longitudinal magnetization recovers between pulses spaced by TR and projects into the receiver plane via sin(theta); maximized at the Ernst angle arccos(e^{-TR/T1}).Using a 90-degree flip angle at short TR, saturating long-T1 gray matter and CSF.
\(e^{-\text{TE}/T_2^*}\)Transverse T2* & Deoxyhemoglobin DecayExponential signal attenuation over echo time TE driven by spin-spin relaxation (1/T2) plus local magnetic field inhomogeneities (gamma Delta B0 from paramagnetic deoxyhemoglobin).Setting TE too short (no BOLD contrast) or too long (total susceptibility dropout near sinuses).

F03 · Read a snippet without becoming a software engineer — Axis-Specific Protected z-Standardization

Removes each voxel's static baseline offset and scales its temporal fluctuation to unit variance while guarding against out-of-brain zero-variance voxels.

\[Z_{v, t} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{X_{v, t} - \mu_v}{\sigma_v} & \text{if } \sigma_v > \epsilon \\[6pt] 0 & \text{if } \sigma_v \le \epsilon \end{cases}, \qquad \mu_v = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} X_{v, t}, \quad \sigma_v = \sqrt{\frac{1}{T - \delta}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(X_{v, t} - \mu_v)^2}\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (F03):
Standardizing an array to mean 0 and standard deviation 1 rescales its units (horizontal axis), but does not change its distribution shape or compute a p-value. Auditing AI code requires reading every axis argument, degree-of-freedom flag (ddof), and division guard.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(X_{v, t} - \mu_v\)Within-Voxel Temporal CenteringSubtracts voxel v's temporal mean across T time points so static T1/T2* baseline brightness is removed.Computing X.mean(axis=0) across voxels instead of axis=1 across time, mixing anatomy with dynamics.
\(\sigma_v \text{ with } \delta \in \{0, 1\}\)Temporal Dispersion & ddof ChoiceDivides by the voxel's temporal standard deviation (population ddof=0 vs. sample ddof=1).Leaving ddof unspecified when comparing code outputs against analytical formulas.
\(\sigma_v > \epsilon \text{ Guard}\)Zero-Variance Numerical Safety GateReplaces 0/0 division in constant background masks with 0.0 instead of propagating NaN/Inf across the volume.Allowing a single masked background voxel to poison downstream matrix multiplications with NaNs.

F04 · A notebook is an experiment with state — Deterministic Computational State Transition

Formalizes a reproducible notebook as a pure composition of state transitions from a fixed random seed s0 and immutable input data.

\[S_k = f_k\!\left(S_{k-1}, \, \mathcal{D}_{\text{immutable}}, \, \text{RNG}(s_0)\right), \qquad \operatorname{SHA256}\!\left(S_K^{\text{fresh}}\right) = \operatorname{SHA256}\!\left(S_K^{\text{saved}}\right)\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (F04):
A Jupyter notebook is an interactive REPL with mutable global state in memory. Out-of-order cell execution, in-place array mutation, and unseeded random number generators destroy reproducibility unless verified in a clean top-to-bottom kernel.
Logical Term Role in Equation What This Part Is Doing (Mechanism & Units) What Breaks If Omitted (Failure Mode)
\(S_{k-1} \to S_k\)Sequential Cell State TransitionEach cell k must depend only on definitions executed in cells 1..k-1 in strict linear order.Running Cell 8 before Cell 4 or deleting a cell whose variable still lingers in RAM.
\(\mathcal{D}_{\text{immutable}}\)Unmutated Input BufferInput arrays are copied or treated as read-only rather than modified in-place across re-runs.In-place operations like X -= X.mean() that change results when a cell is run twice.
\(\text{RNG}(s_0)\)Isolated Random StreamLocal Generator instance np.random.default_rng(s0) supplying deterministic draws independent of global state.Relying on unseeded global np.random calls that shift every time a cell is re-executed.

DS01 · Arrays, axes, broadcasting, and masks — Boolean Mask Indexing & Stride-Aware Tensor Broadcasting

Maps a 4D volume through a 3D spatial mask into a 2D (V_brain, T) matrix and tracks byte stride offsets across array views.

\[Y_{\text{masked}} = \mathcal{M}(Y_{X\times Y\times Z\times T}, M_{X\times Y\times Z}) \in \mathbb{R}^{V_{\text{brain}} \times T}, \qquad \text{offset}(i_0,\dots,i_{d-1}) = \sum_{k=0}^{d-1} s_k \, i_k\]
Diagram & Equation Connection (DS01):
A 4D fMRI array has shape (X, Y, Z, T). Applying a 3D boolean brain mask M of shape (X, Y, Z) compresses the spatial dimensions into a 2D matrix X_masked of shape (V_brain, T). Confusing C-order reshape with transpose scrambles spatial and temporal axes!
