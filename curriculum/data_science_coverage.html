Data-science coverage and assigned source practicals

The 16 notebooks are substantial AI-supervised concept labs aligned to actual source chapters, not a claim to reproduce USC NIIN580's unavailable weekly syllabus. Berkeley Data8 supplies foundational computing/inference coverage; Neuromatch supplies mathematical fitting and representation practicals. Imaging-specific files/metadata are supported by NiBabel's official guide. The imaging and modeling strands extend these foundations.

Coverage depth: each row below has local explanation, an executable known-answer simulation, an AI audit prompt, failure experiment, and an assigned upstream reading/practical. These are small instructional exercises. Full participant-data analysis, learned imputation models, high-dimensional inferential theory, and institution-specific assignments are not claimed completed.

Reuse: Data8 at commit 5235b7653f8dfaeb90e43419b9aa069322f2d60b is CC BY-NC-ND4: linked only, no altered chapters are published. Neuromatch at 44634e960df7a14cd0bf7398187f2d209d26b0e8 is CC BY4 with software additionally BSD3: three unmodified notebooks and notices are in third_party/data_science. Neuromatch precourse has inconsistent README/license labels in the inspected snapshot, so it is not vendored here. New lesson prose/code are independently authored.

Required upstream work for depth

Complete the unmodified Neuromatch model-fitting Tutorials1–2 and geometric-representation Tutorial1 with their original exercises after DS10–14. Use the full upstream dimensionality-reduction day for covariance/PCA details beyond the short simulation. Read Data8's sampling, bootstrap, regression diagnostics, and training/testing chapters in their original hosted form. Their datascience Tables API differs from our pandas API; compare the operations, rather than assuming code is interchangeable.

Added topics relative to the introductory pack

Composite visit keys, NIfTI serialization and scaling contracts, robust summaries, missingness mechanisms, conditional probability/base rates, dependent sampling, participant bootstrap, percent signal change, matrix rank and identifiability, likelihood, optimization/gradient checking, collinearity sensitivity, PCA leakage, full-procedure feature selection, hashes and execution provenance.

Intentional boundaries

No claim of complete mathematical statistics coverage or official course equivalence. Multiple imputation, robust covariance estimators, causal identification, and advanced high-dimensional inference require additional focused study; causal/design and model-evaluation parts are mapped in their separate strands. The learner should complete source exercises and real-data projects before treating the curriculum as research competence.
