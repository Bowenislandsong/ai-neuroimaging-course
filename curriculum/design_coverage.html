Experimental design: source coverage, depth, and remaining gaps

This strand contains 16 standalone teaching notebooks, each with roughly 550–650 words of explanation, small runnable synthetic experiments, numerical assertions, figures, an AI audit prompt, a deliberate failure, a research-transfer exercise, and an answer key. Plan 75–100 minutes per notebook plus time for the written transfer task. The code requires NumPy, SciPy, pandas, and Matplotlib; no data downloads, scanner software, accounts, or large models are needed. AI writes and explains short operations; the learner must defend the estimand, assumptions, units, and validation.

This is a substantial parallel curriculum assembled from actual public courses and research methods materials. It is not an official USC NIIN 520 syllabus, a reconstruction of its weekly teaching, or a claim to replace a master's degree. USC's public curriculum establishes the relevant subject area. The public NIIN 520 syllabus was not located. The course sources below supply real schedules, tables of contents, assignments, and code with independent provenance.

Assessment and progression

After every notebook, the learner must give a two-minute explanation without the AI: target question, input units and hierarchy, transformed quantities, assumptions, one failure, and one independent check. She then submits the transfer artifact named in the notebook. Passing assertions alone is insufficient. For lessons 05–13, the instructor should ask her to translate the toy objects into a real event table, first-level design, contrast image, participant-level table, and corrected result map.

The strand checkpoint is a reviewable analysis plan for a public dataset: estimand, independent unit, event timing, contrast, noise model, exclusions, hierarchy, correction, ROI-selection provenance, power/precision limitations, and an independent replication proposal. A qualified supervisor must review a real study's design and model. These notebooks teach what to inspect and why; their small formulas are not replacements for validated imaging software.

Borrowed material and execution status

Three unmodified upstream notebooks are vendored under third_party/design, together with original licence evidence, exact commit IDs, upstream paths, and SHA-256 hashes. The original notebooks are reference readings with retained historical outputs; they are not part of the offline executable test suite. Legacy APIs and dependencies are documented. Their presence does not imply their old examples pass with the current environment.

Our 16 notebooks are new material and have been run in fresh Python namespaces with Matplotlib/Agg, with all scientific assertions and plots saved. Jupyter UI/kernel integration is a separate environment concern. See design_validation.json for the executed-cell and figure counts. No external data were downloaded by these 16 notebooks. Pinned upstream files and source licence notices remain distinct from newly authored examples.
